Commercial FAQ

Questions about billing, limits, or payments?

Is a credit card required to start the 7-day free trial? +

No. To start the 7-day trial you only need your business name, email, and password. No credit card is required at registration, with zero surprise charges and no automatic renewal.

How does the monthly message limit work? +

The allowance is measured in messages per month. At the limit, the AI assistant stops sending automated replies and new requests are passed to a human operator. You can upgrade to increase the allowance.

How do Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform fees work? +

The Melpis subscription fee covers the software platform and does not include messaging fees charged directly by Meta. These vary by message category (service, utility, marketing) and destination country, billed separately with total transparency and zero Melpis markups.

Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my subscription anytime? +

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel renewal anytime straight from the integrated Stripe billing portal in your settings, with no lock-in periods or termination fees.

How is invoicing handled and does it include European electronic invoicing? +

Your Stripe receipt is available immediately; request a fiscal invoice from us when needed.

Can I manage multiple business locations with a single account? +

Yes. The Scale plan includes native multi-location and multi-number support, enabling you to configure bespoke hours, separate calendars, and distinct operational rules for each location from a unified control panel.
